    vmctx: kill the wakeups -- 61 -> 16 us per forwarded call on loopback; fix the spawn handoff race; the VMX base-read fix
    
    Sessions 45 and 46 (HANDOFF-session45.md, HANDOFF-session46.md).
    
    Performance (session 46). A loopback forwarded syscall was a ring of four
    tasks handing one message round, every hop a sleep and a wake, and the
    sleeping let the frequency governor clock the cores down. Three changes,
    each A/B'd on one boot with arms alternating:
    
      - source kernel #181 (src/linux-7.0.14, patch regenerated): spin before
        sleeping at the four vmctx waits (vmctx_report's reply wait,
        VMCTX_CTL_WAIT, VMCTX_CTL_SYSCALL, the service park), adaptive per site
        and per context, need_resched() bails; vmctx_spin_us (100, 0 = the old
        behaviour), vmctx_spin_hits/misses. 79-113 -> 23-31 us under schedutil.
      - both halves spin before every blocking channel read (VMCTX_SOCK_SPIN_US,
        50, recv(MSG_DONTWAIT) probes, same adaptivity): 25 -> 17.6 us.
      - a request's header and payload in one writev (they were two segments and
        the reader woke twice); the page PUT too.
    
    Final (tests/perf.sh, kernel #181, loopback, schedutil): forwarded syscall
    16 us (was 61), page fault 29-74 us (was 37-89), compute 99.6% of native;
    walls wc 0.041 s (0.051), curl 0.060 (0.083), python3 0.174 (0.313), sha256
    0.135 (0.151), gzip 1.22 (1.35), links2 0.084 (0.114). The cpufreq governor
    was the hidden variance (42-114 vs 35-40 us for the same binaries);
    perf.sh prints it and takes GOV=performance. Readahead on loopback
    re-measured neutral (VMCTX_RA_MIN_RTT_US tunable, default unchanged).
    
    Bugs. #27 (session 45): the VMX guest FS/GS base was read from the VMCS
    after preempt_enable -- read inside vmx_enter_once now. #28: perf.sh's
    native median glued five walls into one line. #29: a spawned context could
    enter before its own monitor was attached (the spawner released, then
    detached; the child's first fetch was an unrecoverable #PF with no monitor
    -- pid1 cross-machine); the spawner now detaches without releasing and the
    child's service thread releases after its ATTACH.
    
    Gates on the final binaries: loopback suite 119/120 (fc1's open residual,
    its trail now read in AUDIT §10), cross suite 40/0, progs 65-67/69 (the
    lifecycle residual families, hit more often at 2-3x the old speed).
